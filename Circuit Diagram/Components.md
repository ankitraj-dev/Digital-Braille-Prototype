# 🔧 Components Used

## *Electronics*
- *ESP32 DevKit v1 (ESP-WROOM-32, 38-pin, USB-C)*
- *Breadboard*
- *Jumper wires*
- *12V mini push-pull solenoids (x6)*
- *P55NF06 N-channel MOSFETs (x6)*
- *1N4001 rectifier diodes (x6)*
- *12V DC power supply (minimum 1.2A)*
- *USB Type-C charger for ESP32*
- *Common ground connection (ESP32 GND ↔ 12V supply GND)*

## *Tools & Accessories*
- *Double-sided tape*
- *Hot glue*
- *Wire stripper*
- *Extra jumper wires*

## *Mechanical Parts*
- *Hard cardboard sheet* (solenoid mounting)
- *Double-sided tape*
- *Hot glue*

## *Computer Requirements*
- *Arduino IDE*
- *ESP32 board manager installed*
- *USB Type-C cable*
- *Bluetooth Terminal App (Android)*  
  (for sending text over Bluetooth SPP)
